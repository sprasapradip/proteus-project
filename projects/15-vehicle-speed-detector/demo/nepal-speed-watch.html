Nepal Speed Watch

Four roadside speed detectors, one in each city. Each one measures every vehicle with two infrared beams 1 m apart, works out its speed, length and class, shows the driver their speed, and keeps the survey numbers a traffic engineer needs.

Simulated traffic. Sites, limits and volumes are demo settings, not official data.
